Privacy

What stays on your device

Your current game, your best games with their replays, your theme and your settings are saved in your browser's local storage on this device. They are never sent anywhere. Clearing this site's data in your browser or removing the app deletes them.

What the game does not do

No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The game loads nothing from other services: no fonts, no scripts and no images from third parties. Once loaded, it works without a connection.

Server logs

When you load the game, the servers delivering it record technical access data: your IP address, the time, the requested file and your browser's identification. These servers are run by {{ operator }}; one of them is rented from Hetzner Online GmbH. The data is needed to deliver the website and keep it secure (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). It is not combined with other data, not used to identify you and deleted regularly.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. Responsible under the GDPR is {{ operator }}, reachable through the contact above.
